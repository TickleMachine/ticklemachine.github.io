The pitch

This city needs a new boss.

Gangsta Style is an action-packed, comedy-tinged game about taking over a city and making it your own.

Go after rival gang bosses, recruit their people and grow your influence until you become the richest and most powerful gangster around. Your empire can be built on ambition, audacity and—when the situation calls for it—some very questionable pigeon training.

Expect action, absurd ideas and a city full of opportunities to make a name for yourself.
