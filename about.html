Why Tickle Machine?

We want to make games that make people feel something.

Sometimes that means getting completely absorbed in a world. Sometimes it means laughing at a plan that has gone spectacularly wrong. We’re building Tickle Machine one idea at a time, with plenty of room to experiment along the way.
